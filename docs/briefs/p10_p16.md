## P15 Onboarding `/empezar`

Owned: `src/app/(public)/empezar/`, `src/components/onboarding/` (new), `src/lib/onboarding/`
(new), `e2e/empezar.spec.ts`. A 4-step guided path with a progress rail: 1) haz el diagnóstico

home/header link.

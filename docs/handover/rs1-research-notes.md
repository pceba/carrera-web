## BIG LAW facts

Needs CV <=2 pages, transcript, cover letter. MCQ areas: mercantil, civil, procesal, societario, público, comunitario, fiscal, laboral. Selection centralized Madrid/Barcelona/Lisbon. Offices in Spain: Barcelona, Bilbao, Madrid, Valencia (+ others). Welcome programme Sept 2025: 82 young professionals.
